WHY THIS RESUBMISSION
1.0.1.0 was returned under 1200.1.1.3 (repo 1.0.0.0 vs package 1.0.1.0). Fixed, and
changelog.html is generated from pbiviz.json, so the two cannot drift. 1.0.3.0 fixes
three audit findings: ids from the data are HTML-escaped before going into attributes;
the licence no longer resolves inside update(); and the purchase path is Power BI's own
notification, not text in the chart.

LICENCE
IVisualLicenseManager only: getAvailableServicePlans() against Plan ID "flow-chart-tcviz",
accepting Active and Warning (grace period). Requested once, deferred, never in the render
path; any failure stays on Free. Where it cannot be queried (Publish to Web, embedding,
export) the free experience renders and nobody is asked to buy.

FREE VS PRO
Free: every feature, capped at 9 nodes kept as a connected sub-tree from the root, with a
neutral "Showing 9 of N nodes" note. No watermark. Pro: the same, every node.

PRIVACY
No network calls, no telemetry, no cookies, no local or session storage. Only the
dataView. Level images only as data:image base64, never fetched. privileges is [].

TESTING - FREE

2. Levels: 3+ fields plus a Value measure, dataset with more than 9 nodes.
3. Expect 9 nodes as a connected sub-tree, the neutral count note, and Power BI's own
